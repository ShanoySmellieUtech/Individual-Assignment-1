Personality Traits

ADVENTUROUS

I have been described as a very adventurous person. I enjoy stepping out of my comfort zone and experiencing new things.

This is important to me because it helps me to free my mind and release school-related stress. It helps me to relax, escape reality, and rejuvenate.

This results in me feeling more refreshed, focused, and prepared for school.

EBULLIENT

I believe that I am a very ebullient person. This trait helps me to keep me grounded and maintain a positive attitude and mindset.

I believe that it is always good to be in high spirits even when facing difficulties.

It helps me to not be disappointed when faced with low grades because I know that one setback doesn't define me.

It also teaches me to be grateful for the opportunity to be in school because not everyone has this opportunity.

To conclude, this mindset helps me to be positive throughout the academic year and encourages me to be a light everywhere I go.

OPTIMISTIC

One of my strongest traits is being optimistic. I believe this trait is important because it often helps me remain positive.

It assures me that despite the hardships that come with school, there is always a bright side and everything will eventually work out.

This trait keeps me motivated and prevents me from being discouraged when I’m challenged with schoolwork. .

Conclusively, it helps me to manage academic pressure and reminds me to keep my head up because I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.

My Mentor

My mentor is my grandfather; he was a successful farmer who spent most of his days toiling in the fields. Farming was his life, not because it was his source of income but because he genuinely enjoyed farming and taking care of his crops and livestock.

He came from a humble background, so just the thought of how far he’s come and the work that he has done inspires me every day to do my best.

What makes him my hero is the fact that he’s always there to encourage me in times of hardship. He always extends a listening ear without judgement and a word of prayer. Although he’s not alive, his words and wisdom continue to guide me. His memory will forever live on in my heart.

One of my favourite quotes that he often said was “If yuh wah good yuh nose affi run” This quote puts into perspective that to do well in life and be successful, you have to work hard.

This encourages me whenever I feel like giving up, I always remember that hard work carries you a far way. His example has taught me to never give up and stay focused on my goals because, with determination, I will achieve them if I believe and work towards them.

LLMs as a crutch

Large language models (LLMs) are advanced artificial intelligence (AI) systems that use machine learning to comprehend and create human-like language (Microsoft, n.d.). “LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool, not a crutch”.

I am a strong believer in this statement. In this technological era, artificial intelligence (AI) has been integrated into today’s educational institutions. Some have completely written AI off.

However, I believe that while it has its disadvantages, it also has advantages. I have experienced that it has undeniably made learning easier. Personally, AI helps me when faced with challenging topics by breaking them down more simply, making outlines, conducting research, checking grammar, paraphrasing, and being used as a study partner; as such, I believe these are acceptable times to use it, where and ways to use it.

On the other hand, I must admit that as a student, I have become overly dependent on these LLMs. I believe there is a need for balance; they should be used as a guide and not be used solely to complete our assignments. As students, we should be able to do our assignments with the knowledge of what we have learnt without relying on AI.

However, due to the easy access to LLMs, students have now forgotten to do simple tasks such as writing essays because they have become accustomed to AI. We have failed to use our brains; our first resort is to use LLMs. I believe this has negatively affected our critical thinking and writing skills.
